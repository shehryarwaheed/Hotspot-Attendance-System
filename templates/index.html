HOW TO REGISTER A STUDENT'S DEVICE

  • Step 1: Ask the student to open their phone's Bluetooth settings screen.
  • Step 2: On THIS laptop → Windows Settings → Bluetooth → Add Device → pair with their phone.
  • Step 3: Once paired, click the 🔄 Refresh Devices button below. Their phone will appear by name.
  • Step 4: Select their Roll Number + their device → click Assign MAC.

✅ This is a ONE-TIME setup per student per semester.
